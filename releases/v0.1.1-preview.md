# VELUM v0.1.1 preview

- Updated clouds, weather transitions, wind and ambience.
- Improved underworld horizon and rendering performance.
- Safer graphics fallback on unsupported hardware.
- Windows and browser builds.

Windows: extract and run `RUN_VELUM.cmd`. [Play in your browser](https://acsokis.github.io/velum/).

Early preview. Hardware support and performance vary. Frame generation and GPU rigid-body physics are unavailable. Third-party license notices are included with the downloads.
