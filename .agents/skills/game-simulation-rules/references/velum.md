# VELUM-specific preferences

- Fully 3D first-person techno-organic exploration/survival; the proposed 2.5D conversion was explicitly canceled.
- Layered PCB terrain retains hills, ramps and elevation. Electronic components form vegetation and environmental objects. Low maze walls should be jumpable and made from PCB/cooling structures with mysterious translucent working fluid.
- Continuous conductive traces lead to plants, props or collectibles. Salvage uses unplugging, desoldering or ripping, with meaningful mechanical consequences.
- Legless functional organisms roll as articulated masses or hover on thrusters. Visible fluid channels conduct coolant or energy; avoid decorative nonfunctional anatomy.
- The procedural sky resembles abstract cable management inside a dusty, webbed server-world, with technological clouds rather than literal fans. Neon pulses run along one cable strand at a time.
- Use seeded corrosion and electronic overgrowth, glitch/matrix weather, discharge flashes and restrained retro/Atari-style atmosphere. Music starts OFF and must be original or properly licensed.
- Rain becomes a signal on impact and follows connected PCB grooves toward actual ecosystem consumers. Thermal fog and actor wakes reflect heat and movement.
- The bounded labyrinth connects PSU, RAM, liquid-cooler and SoC complexes, with entry and escape objectives. Sections may change on traversal/start/load while preserving valid routes and saved progression.
- Spectral/FFT effects may animate the upper sky and underworld electrical gel/whirlpool. Triangular bokeh DOF is optional and must fit the frame budget.
- Prefer the verified NVIDIA Godot native path with supported GI/upscaling. Never claim unsupported VortexGI, Vortex Physics, DLSS or frame generation. Keep a separately optimized playable WebGL path; equal native/web fidelity is a goal, not a verified capability.
- VELUM LINK is a real interactive egg-shell handheld with wrist/forearm/dock transforms and status, map, scanner, probe and messaging panels. The browser companion is optional for gameplay and supports separate simultaneous device panels.
